## Legal working distinction
Visible building/street geometry is usable as observation/reference, but copyrighted photo/video expressions and website platform terms are distinct. Public access is not blanket permission to copy, train, reconstruct, publish or redistribute the creator's media. CC BY-SA photos can be adapted as licensed. For independently authored geometry based on many references, assess any remaining architecture/design/IP and trademark issues separately before commercial release. For an internal research study, review/view online material without presenting it as licensed production footage. No claim of infringement-free publication.
